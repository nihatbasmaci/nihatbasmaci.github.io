Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use and disclosure of Your information when You use Nova IPTV or Apex IPTV (the "Application"), and tells You about Your privacy rights and how the law protects You.

By using the Application, You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

In short: the Application has no accounts and no sign-in. Your playlists, credentials, watch history and favourites stay on your device. We collect usage and crash diagnostics, purchase records needed to restore your Premium access, and — on iPhone and iPad only, and only if you have not purchased Premium — advertising data. We do not sell your personal data.

No Account and No Sign-In

The Application does not require You to create an account with Us, and We do not operate a user account system. Any playlist address, username or password You enter in order to connect to Your own media provider is stored on Your Device and is transmitted only to the provider You have chosen. We do not receive, store or have access to those credentials, and We do not receive the content You play.

Types of Data Collected

Personal Data

We do not ask You to provide personally identifiable information such as Your name, postal address or telephone number, and the Application has no feature that publishes information about You to other users.

Usage and Diagnostic Data

Usage Data is collected automatically when You use the Service. It may include the type and model of Your Device, the operating system version, the version and language of the Application, an application-scoped or device-scoped identifier generated by Us or by our Service Providers, the features and screens You use and when, and crash and performance diagnostics. Our Service Providers may also derive approximate region information from Your Device's Internet Protocol (IP) address.

Purchase Data

When You make an in-app purchase, our subscription Service Provider records the purchase, the selected plan, the transaction status and a device-scoped identifier so that Your Premium access can be recognised and restored. Payment card details are handled entirely by Apple and are never seen by or shared with Us.

Advertising Data (iPhone and iPad only)

If You have not purchased Premium, the Application may display advertisements on iPhone and iPad. Our advertising Service Provider may use Your device advertising identifier and information about the advertisements shown. The Apple TV version of the Application does not display advertisements. Where required, We ask for Your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before any tracking takes place, and You can change that choice at any time in Your Device settings.

Third-Party Services We Use

The Application uses the following Service Providers, each of which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase — usage analytics and crash diagnostics.
  • RevenueCat — managing subscriptions and purchases.
  • Google AdMob — advertising, on iPhone and iPad only.
  • Apple Search Ads attribution — measuring which campaign led to an installation.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — film and series artwork and metadata. Requests contain only the title being looked up.

Use of Your Data

The Company may use the data described above for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the Service, including to monitor its usage and to diagnose faults.
  • For the performance of a contract: to deliver and restore the Premium access You have purchased.
  • To manage Your requests: to attend to and manage requests You send Us.
  • To display advertising to users who have not purchased Premium, on the platforms where advertising is shown.
  • For other purposes such as data analysis, identifying usage trends, determining the effectiveness of our promotional campaigns, and evaluating and improving the Service.

We may share the data described above in the following situations:

  • With Service Providers — with the providers listed above, to operate, analyse and support the Service.
  • For business transfers — in connection with, or during negotiations of, any merger, sale of Company assets, financing or acquisition of all or a portion of Our business.
  • With Your consent — for any other purpose with Your consent.

We do not sell Your personal data.

Your Choices and Deleting Your Data

Because the Application does not operate an account system, most of the data it holds about You is stored on Your Device and is removed when You delete the Application. Playlists, watch history, favourites and settings are stored locally on Your Device.

You may turn off personalised tracking at any time in Your Device settings. You may also contact Us at the address below to ask what data our Service Providers hold in connection with Your Device identifier, and to request its deletion, subject to any legal obligation We have to retain it.
