Frequently asked questionsSık sorulan sorular

I paid but my gems didn't arrive

Gem packs are added to your balance as soon as Apple confirms the purchase. If you were charged but the gems are missing: fully close the game, make sure you're online, then open it again — unfinished purchases are completed on the next launch.

Still missing? Email us with your Apple receipt or order number (iOS Settings → your name → Media & Purchases → Purchase History) and we'll put it right.

I lost my progress / I switched to a new phone

Your progress is saved on your device and backed up to the cloud automatically. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, it comes back by itself as long as iCloud is enabled for Siege Legion.

The safest option is to link your account: Settings → Account → Link with Apple or Unity. Signing in on any device then restores everything. Note that signing in with a different account replaces the progress on that device.

If something is missing, email us your approximate campaign level, any purchases you made and which account you linked.

How do I delete my account?

Inside the game: Settings → Account → Delete Account. See Account & data below for what gets deleted and what to do if you can't open the game.

PvP won't connect or can't find a match

The arena needs an internet connection (the campaign plays offline). If you see "No internet connection" or "Connection timed out": switch between Wi-Fi and cellular, turn off any VPN and try again.

Both players must be on the latest version — an older version can't match with a newer one, so update from the App Store. Matchmaking pairs you by trophy range; at quiet hours Quick Match can take a little longer. You can also play a friend directly with a room code.

Can I get a refund?

All payments are handled by Apple, so refunds go through Apple: open reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID and choose the purchase. We can't issue refunds ourselves.

If Apple declines and you believe that's a mistake, email us and we'll look into it.

How do I report a bug?

Use the Email support button above — the message opens with a ready template. Tell us what you did, what you expected and what happened instead. A screenshot or a short screen recording is worth a thousand words.

Notifications

Siege Legion only sends local reminders from your own device — a chest that's ready, a building that's finished, a shield about to expire. There are no marketing pushes.

Turn them off in the game (Settings → Game → Notifications) or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Siege Legion.

Is the game suitable for my child?

Siege Legion is a fantasy medieval strategy game with stylised battles; its age rating is shown on the App Store page. There are no ads. In-app purchases are optional — you can require approval with Screen Time → Content & Privacy Restrictions → iTunes & App Store Purchases. Clans include a text chat between members.

The game is not directed at children under 13.

İlerlemem kayboldu / yeni telefona geçtim

İlerlemen cihazında saklanır ve otomatik olarak buluta yedeklenir. Yeni iPhone'da ya da yeniden kurulumda, Siege Legion için iCloud açıksa ilerlemen kendiliğinden geri gelir.

En güvenlisi hesabını bağlamak: Ayarlar → Account → Link ile Apple ya da Unity hesabı. Sonra hangi cihazda giriş yaparsan yap her şey geri gelir. Dikkat: farklı bir hesapla giriş yapmak o cihazdaki ilerlemenin yerine geçer.

Bir şey eksikse bize yaklaşık kampanya bölümünü, yaptığın satın almaları ve hangi hesabı bağladığını yaz.

Account & data

You can delete your account from inside the game — no email needed:

  1. Open Settings (the gear icon on the main screen).
  2. Open Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm twice.

This permanently removes your account and cloud save from our servers, and all game progress from this device — levels, gold, gems and upgrades. It cannot be undone, and deleted gems are not refundable. Your device settings (sound, language) are kept, and any linked Apple or Unity sign-in is detached from the deleted account.

Can't open the game? Email us with the subject "Delete account" from the address you'd like us to reply to, and tell us what you can: the sign-in you linked (Apple / Unity), your approximate campaign level and a purchase receipt if you have one. We'll confirm with you and complete the deletion within 30 days.

Request deletion by email

Removing Siege Legion under iOS Settings → your name → Sign in with Apple only revokes the sign-in; it does not delete your game data. Use the steps above for that.

Hesap ve veri

Hesabını oyun içinden silebilirsin — e-posta gerekmez:

  1. Ayarlar'ı aç (ana ekrandaki dişli simgesi).
  2. Account bölümünü aç.
  3. Delete Account'a dokun ve iki kez onayla.

Bu işlem hesabını ve bulut kaydını sunucularımızdan, tüm oyun ilerlemeni de bu cihazdan kalıcı olarak siler — bölümler, altın, elmas ve yükseltmeler. Geri alınamaz; silinen elmaslar iade edilmez. Cihaz ayarların (ses, dil) korunur; bağlı Apple ya da Unity girişi silinen hesaptan ayrılır.

Oyunu açamıyor musun? Yanıt almak istediğin adresten, konusu "Delete account" olan bir e-posta gönder ve elindekileri yaz: bağladığın giriş (Apple / Unity), yaklaşık kampanya bölümün ve varsa bir satın alma makbuzu. Seninle teyitleşip silme işlemini 30 gün içinde tamamlarız.
