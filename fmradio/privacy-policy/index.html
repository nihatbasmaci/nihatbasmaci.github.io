Privacy Policy

FM Radio

Last updated: April 16, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how FM Radio ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile application FM Radio (the "App"). By using the App you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Favorite stations – stored locally on your device.
  • Language preference – stored locally on your device.
  • Last played station – stored locally on your device.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device information – device type, operating system version, language and locale.
  • Usage data – app open count, station searches, playback events.
  • Advertising identifier (IDFA) – collected only after you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt, used to show personalised ads.

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect data independently:

2.1 Google Firebase

  • Firebase Analytics – collects anonymous usage statistics (screen views, events).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging – used to send push notifications.

Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.2 Google AdMob

The App displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your Advertising ID and device information to serve personalised or contextual ads. You can opt out of personalised advertising by revoking the tracking permission via Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking on your device.

Privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

2.3 RevenueCat

In-app purchases and subscriptions are managed by RevenueCat. RevenueCat collects purchase history and subscription status to unlock premium features. No payment card data is processed by us or RevenueCat – all transactions are handled by Apple.

Privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

2.4 Supabase

Radio station data (name, country, genre, stream URL) is fetched from our Supabase backend. Search queries you enter may be transmitted to this server to return relevant results. No personally identifiable information is stored on the server.

3. Microphone Access

The App requests microphone access solely to identify the currently playing song when metadata is unavailable. Audio is processed locally on your device and is not recorded or transmitted to our servers.

4. Push Notifications

With your permission the App may send push notifications (e.g., new station recommendations). You can disable notifications at any time in your device's Settings.

5. Background Audio

The App uses background audio mode to continue radio playback when the screen is off or you switch to another app. No additional data is collected in this mode.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Data Retention

Data stored locally on your device (favorites, preferences) is retained until you delete the App. Analytics data retained by Firebase follows Google's standard retention policies.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. Because most data is stored locally on your device, you can exercise these rights by uninstalling the App or clearing App data in device Settings. For data held by third-party services, please refer to their respective privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by a new "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
