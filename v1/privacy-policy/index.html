Privacy Policy — AI Pet Analyzer

Last updated: October 25, 2025
App Name: AI Pet Analyzer Contact: nihatbasmaci@hotmail.com

1. Introduction

AI Pet Analyzer ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our AI-powered skin analysis application.

The purpose of AI Pet Analyzer is to provide informational skin insights using artificial intelligence — not medical or dermatological diagnosis.

2. Information We Collect

a. Face Images and Camera Access

- The app uses your device's camera to capture an image of your face for skin analysis purposes.

- These images may include visible features such as pores, texture, or pigmentation.

- The app does not collect biometric identifiers (such as facial geometry templates).

b. Device Information

We may collect limited, non-personal information (like device type and operating system version) to improve app performance.

3. How We Use Face Data

- Captured face images are analyzed using OpenAI's GPT-based API (ChatGPT Vision) to generate general, cosmetic-related feedback about your skin condition.

- The analysis is AI-generated and intended for informational purposes only.

- The app does not provide any medical diagnosis or treatment advice.

4. Data Sharing and Processing

- The image is securely transmitted (via HTTPS) to OpenAI's servers for temporary analysis.

- OpenAI acts as a third-party processor for this limited purpose.

- Neither we nor OpenAI store these images permanently or use them to identify individuals.

- Images are immediately deleted after the AI analysis is complete.

For more details about OpenAI’s data handling, please visit: https://openai.com/privacy

5. Data Retention

- Face images are kept temporarily during processing (usually a few seconds).

- After the AI response is received, the image is automatically deleted.

- We do not store or reuse images for model training or analytics.

6. User Consent

By using the app, you agree that:

  • The app may access your camera to capture an image for analysis.
  • The image may be temporarily sent to OpenAI for processing.

You may withdraw consent at any time

Face Data Collection and Retention

AI Pet Analyzer processes face images solely to perform AI-based skin analysis. The analysis detects general skin characteristics (such as acne, pores, and tone) and does not collect or identify any biometric information.

Retention: Face data is not retained after analysis. Each photo is deleted immediately after processing and never stored on our servers or devices.

Third Parties: AI Pet Analyzer does not share face data with any third parties. If external AI processing services are used in the future, they will follow the same policy and delete data immediately after analysis.

Purpose of Collection: Face data is used only to generate a one-time skin analysis report for the user.

Storage Duration: 0 seconds. All images are deleted as soon as the AI analysis completes.

Data Sharing: No face data is stored, shared, or sold to third parties.
