# Apex IPTV demo playlist

Playlist URL: https://nihatbasmaci.github.io/apex-demo/apex.m3u

A small demo catalog for testing and app review of Apex IPTV (Android / Android TV).
Channel and series names are fictional. Video clips are cut from Blender Foundation open
movies (Sintel, Tears of Steel, Cosmos Laundromat, Glass Half and others), licensed
CC BY 3.0 / 4.0 — (c) Blender Foundation | www.blender.org. The three full films are
streamed directly from archive.org.
