Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes what information Nova IPTV and Apex IPTV (the "Application") collect, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what choices and rights You have. The Application is published by Nihat Basmacı, an individual developer based in Turkey ("We", "Us", "Our"). We are the data controller for the processing described here.

By using the Application, You agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

In short: the Application has no accounts and no sign-in. Your playlists, credentials, watch history and favourites stay on Your Device (and, if You leave iCloud sync on, in Your own iCloud). We collect usage and crash diagnostics, connection-failure diagnostics and the purchase records needed to restore Your Premium access. If You open the in-app support chat, what You type is stored so that We can answer. If You turn on the optional AI features, channel names from Your playlist are sent to an AI provider. The Application shows no advertisements, uses no advertising identifier and does not track You across other apps or websites. We do not sell Your personal data.

The Application contains no advertising of any kind. What We Collect and Who Receives It

Everything below is listed per Service Provider so that You can see exactly what leaves Your Device. Each provider processes data under its own privacy policy.

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics — receives the model of Your Device, the operating system version, the version and language of the Application, a Firebase app-instance identifier, which screens and features You use and when, crash and performance diagnostics, whether the Device has Premium, and the host name (never the username or password) of the playlist server You connect to, which We use to understand connection failures. Google may derive an approximate region from Your IP address. You can turn this off (see "Your Choices").
  • Google Firestore (Our own database) — receives (a) a connection-error record when a playlist fails to load: time, error type, the playlist address with credentials removed, the host name, the HTTP status, the Application version, the network type (Wi-Fi or cellular) and a pseudonymous device hash; at most 20 such records per Device per day; and (b) the support chat described in "In-App Support Chat".
  • RevenueCat — manages in-app purchases. It records the purchase, the selected plan, the transaction status and a device-scoped identifier so that Your Premium access can be recognised and restored. It also receives the Firebase app-instance identifier and the Apple Search Ads attribution token. Payment card details are handled entirely by Apple and are never seen by or shared with Us or RevenueCat.
  • Apple Search Ads attribution and SKAdNetwork — on first launch the Application asks Apple for an attribution token and reports conversion values through Apple's SKAdNetwork. This tells Us which campaign, if any, led to the installation. It does not identify You and is not tracking.
  • The Movie Database (TMDB) — to show artwork and descriptions for films and series, the Application sends the titles in Your playlist to TMDB as search queries. No identifier is sent. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
  • ESPN and EuroLeague (sports scores feature) — the Matches screens request fixtures, scores, standings and team or player information from ESPN's and EuroLeague's public endpoints. No identifier is sent. Team and league names and logos are the property of their owners; We are not affiliated with ESPN, EuroLeague or any league.
  • Anthropic (Claude) through Our Cloudflare Worker (AI features, opt-in) — nothing is sent until You turn on "AI features" in Settings and accept the notice shown there. After that, to organise Your live channels, the Application sends the channel names and group names from Your playlist (never stream addresses, usernames or passwords) to a small relay service We run on Cloudflare, which forwards the text to Anthropic's Claude model. The relay stores a daily request counter per Device (deleted after 2 days), a salted hash of Your IP address used only to limit abuse (deleted after 10 minutes), and a shared cache of channel classifications that is not linked to any Device. We send no identifier to Anthropic; Anthropic processes the text under its own policy. Devices without Premium never make this call.
  • Firebase App Check (Apple DeviceCheck) — before the AI call above, the Application obtains an attestation from Apple that proves the request comes from a genuine copy of the Application. It contains no personal data.
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit) — optional, on by default, controlled under Settings › Your devices. Your favourites, watchlist, watch history and resume positions, series progress and Your playlists (including Xtream usernames and passwords, which are stored in CloudKit's encrypted fields) are synchronised between Your own devices through Your own iCloud private database. The data lives in Your iCloud account; We cannot read it. Turn it off in Settings; delete it with "Delete my data" in Settings, or by removing the Application's iCloud data in iOS Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.

In-App Support Chat

The Application has a built-in support chat (Settings › Help › Support). It exists so that You can ask Us for help without an account or an e-mail client. When You send a message, the text You typed is stored in Our Firestore database together with a device record: the Application version and build, the operating system version, the Device model, the language and region, whether the Device has Premium, the type of playlist You use (M3U or Xtream) and the type of the last connection error. Text that looks like a link or a credential is masked before it is sent, but please do not type personal data into the chat.

Each Device has one conversation, identified by a random 128-bit identifier stored in the Device Keychain. The identifier is the only key to the conversation: anyone who holds it could read the thread, but conversations cannot be listed or searched by anyone other than Us. You can delete the whole conversation at any time from Settings › Privacy › Delete my data. Otherwise We keep it for up to 12 months after the last message.

Identifiers

The Application generates three random identifiers and stores them in the Device Keychain: a purchase identifier used by RevenueCat, the support conversation identifier and an identifier for the AI relay. They are not derived from You or Your Apple Account, but they survive deleting and reinstalling the Application. A short hash derived from the purchase identifier (the "device hash") is attached to the connection-error diagnostics and to the support device record so that We can match a support request with the errors that Device reported. The hash is pseudonymous, not anonymous: it does not name You, but it consistently identifies one Device. "Delete my data" in Settings resets all three identifiers.

Your Choices

  • Share usage analytics (Settings › Privacy) — turn off to stop Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics from collecting anything on this Device.
  • AI features (Settings) — off by default. Turn on to enable the AI channel organiser; turn off to stop all AI calls.
  • Your devices (Settings) — turn iCloud sync off to keep Your data on this Device only.
  • Delete my data (Settings › Privacy) — deletes Your support conversation from Our database, deletes the Application's iCloud sync data from Your iCloud, and resets the identifiers above. Connection-error diagnostics cannot be deleted one by one, because they are not searchable by Device; they expire automatically after 90 days.
  • Deleting the Application removes everything stored on the Device. Data in Your iCloud remains until You delete it; Keychain identifiers survive reinstallation unless You used "Delete my data" first.

You may also contact Us at the address below to ask what Our Service Providers hold in connection with Your Device identifiers and to request its deletion, subject to any legal obligation We have to retain it.
